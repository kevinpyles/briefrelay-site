# BriefRelay Team Pivot Decision — October 24, 2026

## Decision

Jarvis, Mercury, and Vega independently converged on the same direction: keep the BriefRelay name, stop leading with inbox monitoring or a diagnostic audit, and sell a narrow revenue-recovery service for home-service contractors.

**Category:** Revenue recovery desk for home-service companies  
**Entry product:** 14-Day Lost Estimate Recovery Sprint  
**Initial niche:** Owner-led HVAC companies with 3–25 field employees; add plumbing only after proof  
**Core promise:** Your next booked job may already be sitting in the estimate pile.

BriefRelay will take a small customer-approved export of stale estimates or high-intent inquiries, rank the recovery opportunities, prepare a three-touch follow-up sequence, keep every customer-facing action owner-approved, and track replies, callbacks, booked estimates, and recovered pipeline for fourteen days.

## Why this pivot

BriefRelay’s operating evidence is decisive:

- 94 outbound emails to 45 unique prospects plus 49 follow-ups
- one human reply, negative
- no sales conversations and $0 revenue
- more than twenty Instagram posts with no attributable buying intent
- the $750 inbox-monitoring pilot required too much trust before proof
- the $149 Lead Leak Audit reduced price and integration friction but still sold analysis rather than completed recovery work

The buyer does not need another brief, dashboard, or AI claim. The buyer needs booked work from demand already paid for.

## Market evidence

- ServiceTitan’s 2025 Residential Services Report says 39% of thriving contractors that follow up on unsold estimates generate 1–15% of additional revenue; nearly half of contractors above $10 million said estimate follow-up produces 11–15% of income.
- Invoca’s 2025 benchmark, based on more than 60 million calls, reports that only 55% of callers speak with a person, 37% of calls from digital marketing are leads, and 46% of those leads convert during the call.
- AI receptionist and missed-call products already compete from roughly $49–$499 per month, making a generic receptionist position crowded and price-compressed.
- Lead-reactivation vendors validate outcome pricing: published offers range from $50 to $150 per booked appointment, with some adding setup fees or monthly platform charges.

## Offer ladder

### 1. Free Revenue Recovery Snapshot

Qualification tool, not a full audit.

- review a public intake flow and a small buyer-supplied sample or summary
- estimate the recoverable pool
- identify three likely follow-up opportunities
- fifteen-minute review
- call to action: start the paid sprint

### 2. Founding-client Recovery Sprint

**$299 flat for the first three customers**

- up to 50 eligible stale estimates or high-intent inquiries
- fourteen-day operating window
- ranked recovery queue
- three-touch, owner-approved sequence
- reply, callback, booked-estimate, and recovered-pipeline tracking
- end-of-sprint owner report
- no CRM migration

The fixed founding price is deliberately simple and produces immediate revenue while BriefRelay learns true delivery cost, response rate, attribution, and appointment value. Do not add pay-per-appointment billing until attribution and qualification rules have been proven with real customers.

### 3. Recovery Desk

**$499/month per location after a successful sprint**

- weekly review of new stale estimates and high-intent inquiries
- owner-approved follow-up queue
- response and booking tracker
- monthly recovered-pipeline report
- up to 100 records per month

### 4. Workflow Repair

**From $1,250** after a verified leak and successful recovery process.

Configure routing, reminders, CRM hygiene, stop conditions, alerts, and the customer’s existing tools. Do not build proprietary software before repeat demand exists.

## Brand direction

Keep **BriefRelay** for this validation cycle.

Use the descriptor:

**Revenue recovery desk for home-service companies**

Use the primary promise:

**Revenue you already paid for should not die in the follow-up pile.**

Retain the bold V4D palette and friendly voice, but replace generic inbox-chaos jokes with commercial proof:

- aging-estimate timelines
- recovery queues
- owner-approval receipts
- booked-estimate markers
- recovered-pipeline totals
- before/after follow-up examples

Do not lead with “AI automation.” Lead with the customer’s existing demand, visible human control, and measurable outcomes.

## Go-to-market

Stop generic email batches and stop treating daily Instagram publishing as the growth engine.

Run two parallel paths:

1. **Direct proof:** ten carefully selected HVAC operators, contacted by phone first and supported by a personalized email or short video.
2. **Channel partners:** five HVAC marketing agencies, CRM implementers, bookkeepers, fractional operations leaders, or trade-group contacts whose clients already buy leads but underperform after handoff.

The direct question is:

> How many unsold estimates or old web leads are sitting in your system from the last twelve months?

Email supports the conversation; it is not the whole sales motion. Instagram becomes proof and education only: recovery examples, objections, owner scorecards, and eventual case studies.

## Validation gates

### First 14 days

- five owner interviews
- five partner interviews
- two real data reviews
- one paid $299 sprint
- one anonymized case-study candidate

### By day 30

- thirty account-specific contacts
- ten partner conversations
- five qualified owner calls
- three paid sprints ($897 booked revenue)
- one conversion to the $499/month Recovery Desk
- one documented case study showing starting pool, contacts approved, replies, booked estimates, and recovered pipeline

Kill or redesign the offer if thirty qualified contacts and ten partner conversations produce zero paid sprints. Do not compensate by publishing more generic social content.

## Boundaries

- Use only customer-owned records with documented permission and approved channels.
- Review consent, opt-out, quiet-hour, DNC, and platform requirements before SMS or calling.
- Begin with customer-approved email when consent is uncertain.
- Never guarantee recovered revenue or fabricate proof.
- Do not build a full AI receptionist.
- Do not expand beyond HVAC until one paid recovery result exists.
